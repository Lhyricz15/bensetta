A letter from my heart

To my Rosetta,

A few words for the woman who has a special place in my story.

My dearest Rosetta Nana Adwoa Fosimaa Amponsah,

Happy third anniversary, my love. When I look back to the beginning of our story, I feel grateful that life brought us to the same chapter. Since 27 October 2023, we have been writing something beautiful, gentle, and deeply ours.

I love the way we grow together—how you make life feel lighter, kinder, and more meaningful. You have taught me that love is not only the big, romantic moments, but also the ordinary ones: the patience, the effort, the listening, the quiet support, and the joy of simply being known.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise to keep learning how to love you well: to listen, to respect you, to celebrate your wins, to stand beside you through hard days, and to welcome every chapter of us with gratitude.

May the years ahead bring us deeper understanding, good health, peace, joy, growth, and countless reasons to smile at the life we are building. May we never become too busy to appreciate each other. May we keep choosing kindness, patience, and tenderness in all the small ways that make a relationship strong.

Thank you for being part of my life and for sharing this journey with me. Three years are a beautiful milestone, but in my heart they are also a reminder: there is still so much love to give, so much life to live, and so many more memories to make together.

Today, tomorrow, and in all the chapters still to come, I hope we keep making room for kindness, friendship, patience, romance, and the joy of simply being us.

With all my love,

Benjamin ✦
